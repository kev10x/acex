# MarkMate: AI-Powered Assignment Marking for High Schools

## What is MarkMate?

MarkMate is an intelligent marking system that uses artificial intelligence to help teachers grade student assignments quickly and consistently. Upload student work and a rubric (or marking guide), and MarkMate provides detailed feedback, scores, and suggestions—just like having a teaching assistant working alongside you.

## How It Works

**1. Upload Your Rubric**  
   - Upload your marking rubric or answer key (PDF or Word document)
   - MarkMate can even generate a rubric from your assignment instructions
   - The system automatically detects if you've uploaded an answer key/memorandum

**2. Upload Student Work**  
   - Upload individual assignments or batch process multiple submissions
   - Works with typed PDFs and scanned/handwritten documents
   - Supports question papers, essays, reports, and more

**3. Get Instant Feedback**  
   - AI analyzes each submission against your rubric
   - Provides detailed comments for each criterion
   - Suggests improvements and highlights strengths
   - Generates PDF reports with annotations

**4. Review and Adjust**  
   - Review all feedback before finalizing grades
   - Edit scores and comments as needed
   - Export results to CSV or download annotated PDFs

## Support for Handwritten Work

MarkMate can process scanned assignments and handwritten submissions, making it perfect for traditional paper-based assessments. The system automatically detects when standard text extraction isn't sufficient and uses advanced image recognition to read handwritten content.

## MCQ Form Processing

MarkMate includes a dedicated MCQ (Multiple Choice Question) form processor that:

- **Automatically extracts answers** from scanned bubble sheets using AI vision
- **Compares against answer keys** for instant grading
- **Processes multiple forms** in batch
- **Provides detailed results** with scores and percentages
- **Exports to CSV** for easy gradebook integration

Simply upload your answer key (e.g., "1. A, 2. B, 3. C...") and the scanned MCQ forms, and get instant results with no manual marking needed.
