QUESTION 1: Define DevOps and explain its key principles

Max Points: 10

Model Answer:
DevOps is a set of practices that combines software development (Dev) and IT
operations (Ops) to shorten the development lifecycle and provide continuous
delivery with high quality.

Key principles that must be covered:
1. Collaboration between development and operations teams
2. Automation of processes (CI/CD pipelines)
3. Continuous monitoring and feedback
4. Infrastructure as Code
5. Microservices architecture

Marking Scheme:
- Full Marks (9-10 points): Complete definition with all 5 principles 
  explained clearly and accurately
- Good (7-8 points): Definition with 3-4 principles covered, minor gaps 
  or slight inaccuracies
- Fair (5-6 points): Basic definition with 2-3 principles, some 
  inaccuracies or missing key concepts
- Poor (0-4 points): Incomplete or incorrect definition, missing most 
  principles, or fundamental misunderstandings

QUESTION 2: Calculate deployment frequency

Max Points: 5

Model Answer:
Given: 120 deployments in 30 days

Calculation:
Deployment frequency = Total deployments / Number of days
= 120 / 30
= 4 deployments per day

Alternative acceptable formats:
- 120 ÷ 30 = 4
- 4 deployments/day
- 4 deployments per day

Marking Scheme:
- Full Marks (5 points): Correct calculation showing work, answer is 
  4 deployments per day
- Partial (3 points): Correct formula used but arithmetic error in 
  calculation
- Partial (1 point): Shows understanding of concept but incorrect 
  approach or formula
- No Marks (0 points): Incorrect answer or no attempt

QUESTION 3: Compare monolithic and microservices architectures

Max Points: 15

Model Answer:
A comprehensive comparison should cover three main areas:

1. Monolithic Architecture Characteristics:
   - Single, unified application containing all functionality
   - All components tightly coupled together
   - Easier initial development and deployment
   - Harder to scale and maintain as application grows
   - Uses single technology stack
   - Single deployment unit

2. Microservices Architecture Characteristics:
   - Multiple independent services working together
   - Loosely coupled components
   - More complex initial setup and coordination
   - Easier to scale and maintain individual services
   - Can use multiple technology stacks
   - Independent deployment of services

3. Key Differences:
   - Deployment: Monolithic = single deployment, Microservices = 
     independent deployments
   - Scalability: Monolithic = scale entire app, Microservices = scale 
     individual services
   - Technology: Monolithic = single stack, Microservices = multiple 
     stacks possible
   - Team structure: Monolithic = single team, Microservices = multiple 
     teams per service
   - Fault tolerance: Monolithic = single point of failure, Microservices 
     = isolated failures

Marking Scheme:
- Excellent (13-15 points): Comprehensive comparison covering all three 
  sections with clear explanations, examples, and accurate information
- Good (10-12 points): Good comparison covering most aspects from 2-3 
  sections, may miss some details or have minor inaccuracies
- Fair (7-9 points): Basic comparison covering 1-2 sections but missing 
  significant aspects or has notable inaccuracies
- Poor (0-6 points): Inadequate comparison, missing most key points, 
  incorrect information, or fundamental misunderstandings
