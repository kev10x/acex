Authoring Rules
1. Keep each slide marker on its own line, starting with Slide N:.
2. Use ascending numbers with no gaps.
3. Keep title and key point concise.
4. Avoid decorative separators that break the Slide N pattern.
5. If content is very long, split into multiple slide markers instead of long paragraphs.
